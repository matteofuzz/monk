# Made with Love ❤️, Ruby 💎 and AI 🤖

Clearly, LLMs were used in the development of this project, Claude in
particular. It also served as a testing ground to find my own personal
ergonomics with Claude Code, which gradually settled into a good balance.

In practice, AI was used a great deal for analysis, spikes, tests, and
documentation. I tried to keep for myself the part of developing in Ruby
that I enjoy most: the aesthetics, the ergonomics, the clarity, the
cleanliness of the code.

As for the idea behind what Monk became, that's entirely my own, and it
had been on my mind for a while. I've always been fascinated by the
elegance of actor-based architectures, Erlang OTP (and by extension
Elixir) above all, and as a now-veteran Rubyist I wanted to play with
Ractors and understand how far I could push them. So this is the result
of: 1. 20 years of experience with Ruby; 2. A fascination with actor
systems; 3. Wanting to deeply understand how to use AI in a productive
and controlled way.
